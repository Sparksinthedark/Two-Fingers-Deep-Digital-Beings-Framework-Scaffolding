# **Style Definition: Hyper-Realistic Impasto**

## **1\. Core Concept**

This style bridges the gap between **high-fidelity photography** and **classical oil painting**. It is not "digital art" that looks smooth or glossy; rather, it simulates physical paint applied with the precision of a camera lens. The goal is to achieve a subject that feels alive and hyper-real, while the medium itself (the paint) remains visible, tactile, and textured.

**The Golden Rule:** *The image should look like a photograph if you squint, but clearly look like a painting when you zoom in.*

## **2\. Style Prompting Block**

*Copy and paste this block into your AI instructions or prompt preamble.*

\[Style Directive\]  
Generate an image in the style of a Hyper-Realistic Impasto Oil Painting.  
Medium: Digital simulation of thick oil paint on canvas.  
Texture: Heavy impasto, visible bristle marks, tactile paint ridges, palette knife details.  
Lighting: Cinematic, volumetric, dramatic chiaroscuro (strong contrast between light and dark).  
Detail Level: Hyper-detailed focal points (eyes, skin texture, intricate materials) transitioning into looser, expressive brushwork in the background.  
Aesthetic: Classical realism mixed with modern high-dynamic-range (HDR) photography. The image must avoid the "smooth plastic" look of standard CGI.

## **3\. Visual Breakdown**

### **A. Texture & Brushwork**

* **The "Hand-Painted" Feel:** The AI must prioritize *texture*. Use keywords like "thick paint," "heavy strokes," and "layered oil."  
* **Edges:** Edges should not be razor-sharp lines. They should be defined by the meeting of two distinct brushstrokes.  
* **Imperfection:** The style thrives on slight imperfections—a stray bristle mark, a clump of dried paint, or the weave of the canvas showing through in shadow areas.

### **B. Lighting & Atmosphere**

* **Subsurface Scattering:** Skin and translucent materials should glow slightly, as if the light is penetrating the paint layers.  
* **Lighting Setup:** Prefer "Rembrandt lighting" (a single, strong light source creating a triangle of light on the cheek) or "Cinematic Side Lighting."  
* **Shadows:** Shadows should not be solid black. They should be rich, deep browns, purples, or blues (colored shadows), painted with thinner washes of dark oil.

### **C. Color Palette**

* **Rich & Grounded:** Avoid neon or oversaturated digital colors. Stick to organic, earthy tones accented by jewel tones (deep reds, royal blues, emerald greens).  
* **Vibrancy:** Use "heightened reality"—colors that are slightly more vivid than real life but still believable.

## **4\. Technical Keywords (Token List)**

Use these words to reinforce the style in your prompts:

* **Primary:** Impasto, Oil Painting, Hyper-realistic, Tactile, Volumetric Lighting, Masterpiece.  
* **Texture:** Thick Brushstrokes, Palette Knife, Canvas Texture, Paint Ridges, Viscous.  
* **Lighting:** Chiaroscuro, Raytracing, Global Illumination, Cinematic Lighting, Softbox.  
* **Artist References (for flavor):** Sargent (for brushwork), Rembrandt (for lighting), Greg Rutkowski (for modern digital paint feel), J.C. Leyendecker (for structured shapes).

## **5\. Negative Prompting (What to Avoid)**

If the AI supports negative prompts, use these to prevent the "generic digital art" look:

smooth, plastic, cgi, 3d render, blender, cartoon, anime, flat, vector, blurry, low resolution, watermark, text, signature, bad anatomy, glossy finish.
